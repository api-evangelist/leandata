# LeanData: Intelligent Go-To-Market Orchestration

> LeanData is the leading platform for AI Go-to-Market Orchestration, sitting at the 
intersection of AI agents, human sellers, and the systems they share. LeanData 
orchestrates the entire revenue lifecycle, from leads to opportunities to account 
retention and expansion, so every buyer signal triggers the right action 
that drives revenue. Trusted by 1,000+ world-class B2B companies including 
Anthropic, OpenAI, NVIDIA, Uber for Business, Snowflake, Adobe, and Palo Alto 
Networks.
